## Introduction

Osoora AI Chatbot is a powerful and intuitive application that allows you to have conversations with an advanced AI. You can ask questions, get information, and even get help with creative tasks. All your conversations are saved, so you can easily pick up where you left off.

## Getting Started

To use the Osoora AI Chatbot, you'll need to create an account.

### Registering an Account

1.  Open the application and click on the **Sign In** button in the user menu.
2.  You will be redirected to the account page. Click on the **"Don't have an account? Sign up"** link.
3.  Fill in your desired username, email address, and password.
4.  Click the **Sign Up** button. You will be automatically logged in and redirected to the chat page.

### Logging In

1.  If you are not already logged in, click on the **Sign In** button in the user menu.
2.  Enter your email address and password.
3.  Click the **Login** button.

## The Chat Interface

The chat interface is simple and easy to use. Here's a quick overview:

*   **Chat History (Left Sidebar):** This is where all your past conversations are listed. You can switch between them or start a new one from here.
*   **Chat Window (Main Area):** This is where your conversation with the chatbot takes place.
*   **Message Input (Bottom):** Type your message here and press Enter or click the send button to send it.
*   **User Menu (Top Right):** Access your profile settings, change the theme, or log out from here.

## Sending Messages

To start a conversation with the chatbot, simply type your message in the input field at the bottom of the screen and press **Enter** or click the **Send** button. The chatbot's response will appear in the chat window.

The chatbot supports Markdown, so you may see formatted text, lists, and even code blocks in its responses.

## Managing Conversations

### Starting a New Chat

To start a new conversation, click the **New Chat** button in the chat history sidebar. A new, empty chat will be created, and you can start typing your message.

### Switching Between Conversations

All your past conversations are saved in the chat history sidebar. To view a previous conversation, simply click on it in the list. The chat window will update with the selected conversation's messages.

### Deleting a Conversation

To delete a conversation, hover over it in the chat history sidebar and click the **delete** icon (a trash can). You will be asked to confirm before the conversation is permanently deleted.

## Customizing Your Experience

### Changing the Theme

You can switch between a light and dark theme to suit your preference.

1.  Click on the user menu in the top right corner.
2.  Click on the **theme toggle** (a sun or moon icon) to switch between light and dark mode.

## Managing Your Account

### Updating Your Profile

You can update your username and password from the account page.

1.  Click on the user menu and select **Profile Settings**.
2.  On the account page, you can change your username or update your password.
3.  Click **Update Profile** to save your changes.

### Logging Out

To log out of your account, click on the user menu and then click the **Sign Out** button.
